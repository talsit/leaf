# NEON SKYHOOK

An 80s-neon helicopter rescue game: fly a chopper through a night-time city, pick people up with your skid, and carry the swinging human chain to a hospital roof.

**Play:** https://talsit.github.io/leaf/

- Arrow keys / WASD to fly (on a phone: put your thumb anywhere and slide). `M` toggles sound.
- Casts: people, creatures, friendly animals, just fat people, just kids. Standard (24) or Mega (240).
- Invincible and no-crash options, the INFERNO boss level (type `boss` on the menu), and the DOGGO SPHERE world.

Single self-contained `index.html` (three.js loaded from a CDN). Earlier versions are kept as `index00.html`, `index01.html`, …
